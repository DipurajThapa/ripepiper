## Managed journey and ownership (M02-D)

The website advertises four on-demand managed packages, with a public consultation at `site/book.html` posting `/api/lead` then `/api/book`. That is Ripe Piper's own intake, separate from client campaign appointment handoff. Staff discovery and setup precede client activity; the present admin and portal cover invitations, reporting/proposals and quote actions, but there is no complete operator configuration workflow for the four engines. The phrase “after you sign up” in `site/how-it-works.html` and existing quote/payment flows need a later scoped copy/flow review, not deletion in this audit.

## Data flows and notice gaps (M02-E)

`site/book.html` collects name, work email, website, package, country, deal band and a consent checkbox; the Worker stores it in `leads` with `tenant_id IS NULL`, logs source/UTM and may alert the operator through Gmail. Booking then creates `meetings`, a Google Calendar/Meet event and Gmail confirmation. Calculator and newsletter have separate inputs/consent; opt-out is a separate public route. Portal/admin use browser Supabase Auth/PostgREST; the Worker uses a server-side service role. Proposals, quotes and payments can involve Stripe/Razorpay; jobs may call Google Sheets/Drive, WhatsApp, Anthropic and ad APIs if configured. Browser script stores UTM/gclid/fbclid in `sessionStorage`; Google Fonts and Turnstile are external resources. No marketing pixel or analytic implementation was confirmed by the inspected source search, but absence of all third-party tracking has not been independently proven. No verified deployed Worker or configured external keys means no operational data flow was observed in this audit.

`site/privacy.html` exists but contains `[legal entity name]` and `[registered address]` placeholders and claims privacy-friendly analytics, regular backups, specific retention periods, permanent suppression, 30-day response and 48-hour opt-out without operational evidence here. `site/book.html` also displays a placeholder WhatsApp number, a one-hour reply claim and a promised free sample. These are claim/identity review blockers before a live pilot. The code records `consent_at`, but current schema/implementation does not establish per-campaign jurisdiction/channel/source rights, legal-role allocation, choice evidence, retention deletion or subject-request execution. No client compliance brief, processor/transfer review, approved market/channel profile or real campaign authorization is recorded. Do not infer legal status from these gaps; CTS-01–08 remain unresolved for live activity. Ripe Piper as first customer must go through the same profile and tenant controls.
