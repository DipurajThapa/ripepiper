About Ripe Piper

Ripe Piper books qualified sales meetings for B2B companies in the UAE and India. We are a small, founder-led team based in Dubai.

DT

Dipuraj Thapa

Founder

Dipuraj has spent more than 20 years leading operations and delivery teams for Fortune 500 companies. In that time he saw the same problem in many businesses: good leads were collected, then forgotten, because nobody owned the follow-up.

He started Ripe Piper to fix that problem with a clear process: find the right companies, write to them in plain language, reply quickly and hand over only the buyers who are ready to talk.

Every client works directly with Dipuraj during setup, and he reviews every client's results each month.

Why the name

Why we are called Ripe Piper.

"Ripe" describes a lead that is ready to buy. "Piper" refers to a person who leads others by playing a tune. Our job is to find the leads that are ready and bring them to your sales team.

Our rules

Six rules we follow with every client.

01Plain words

We write short, clear messages that a busy person can understand in ten seconds.

02Your approval

We send nothing in your name until you have approved it.

03Real numbers

We report delivered emails, replies and meetings held, using the definitions on our How it works page. We never publish results or reviews that we have not earned.

04Clean data

We never buy contact lists. We contact business addresses only and keep a permanent do-not-contact list.

05Fast replies

A person answers every interested reply within one hour during business hours.

06WhatsApp by permission

We send WhatsApp messages only to people who contacted you first. We never send cold WhatsApp messages.
