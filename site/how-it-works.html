How Ripe Piper turns leads into booked meetings

This page explains each step of our process, what we need from you, what happens in your first 14 days, and the results we publish every week.

Book a 15-minute callarrow_forward
The four steps

The same four steps apply to every package.

01

We find the right companies

We collect leads from your old records, your new inquiries, event contacts or public buying signals. We give every lead a score from 0 to 100.

02

We write a short, specific message

Each message refers to that company's real situation, such as the quote they requested or the job they posted.

how_to_regYou approve
03

We send at a safe daily volume

We send from warmed-up inboxes to verified addresses, and every email has an unsubscribe option.

04

We book the meeting

A member of our team answers every interested reply within one hour and books the meeting on your calendar.

support_agentA person replies
Lead scores

We score every lead from 0 to 100.

The score tells your team which leads to speak to first. It combines two things: how closely the company matches your ideal customer, and how recently it showed interest or a buying signal.

Cold: score 0–39 Warming: score 40–74 Ready: score 75–100
ScoreLabelWhat we do
0–39ColdWe keep the lead on file and contact it only if a new signal appears.
40–74WarmingWe send a short, specific message and up to two follow-ups.
75–100ReadyWe prioritise the lead and offer a meeting time in our first reply.
Your control

You decide at the two points that affect your reputation.

how_to_reg

Approval before sending

For the first 30 days, you approve every message in a shared review sheet before we send it. After 30 days, you can choose to approve only messages to your most important accounts.

support_agent

A person handles every reply

Our software sorts replies, but a member of our team reads and answers every interested or unhappy reply within one hour during business hours.

Your first 14 days

What happens after you sign up.

Day 1

Kick-off call (30 minutes)

We agree on your ideal customer, the meeting times you offer and the rules for a qualified meeting. We confirm everything in writing.

Days 1–2

You share your data

You send us your lead list or connect your forms. You also share your calendar link so we can book meetings.

Days 2–3

We clean and verify the list

We remove duplicates, invalid addresses and anyone who has asked not to be contacted. We then score every lead.

Day 3

You approve the first messages

We send you the first batch of draft messages. Nothing is sent until you approve them.

Days 4–13

Sending and replies

We send at a safe daily volume, follow up twice and answer every interested reply within one hour.

Day 14

Review call

We show you the numbers: messages sent, replies, meetings booked and meetings held. We agree on the next step together.

What we need from you

Four things from your side.

  • check_circleA description of your ideal customer and the job titles you want to meet.
  • check_circleYour lead list, or access to the forms that collect your inquiries.
  • check_circleA booking link to your calendar, or a list of available time slots each week.
  • check_circleAbout 15 minutes a day in the first two weeks to approve messages.
How we protect your reputation

Six safety rules we always follow.

  • shieldWe send cold email from separate domains, never from your main company domain.
  • shieldWe set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC records on every sending domain.
  • shieldWe verify every email address before the first send.
  • shieldWe start at 20 to 30 emails per inbox per day and increase slowly.
  • shieldEvery email includes an unsubscribe option, and we honour requests within 48 hours.
  • shieldWe keep a permanent do-not-contact list across all clients.

How we measure

What each number means.

MeasureDefinition
DeliveredEmails that did not bounce back as undeliverable.
Reply rateReplies of any kind divided by emails delivered. Automatic out-of-office replies are not counted.
Interested repliesReplies that ask for information, a price or a meeting.
Meetings heldMeetings that took place with a person who matches the agreed ideal customer description. Meetings the buyer did not attend are not counted.
First reply timeTime between a new inquiry arriving and our first reply. We report the median, which is the middle value of all replies that week.
