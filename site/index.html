Questions

Answers to the questions clients ask most.

Will cold outreach damage my company's email reputation?add

No. Under our Managed plan we send from separate domains that we set up and warm up for this purpose. Your main company domain does not send any cold email, so its reputation is not affected.

Who writes the messages?add

Our system writes a first draft for each lead based on that lead's situation, and a member of our team edits it. For the first 30 days, you approve every message before we send it.

What counts as a qualified meeting?add

Before we start, you and we agree in writing on your ideal customer: industry, company size, location and the job titles you want to meet. A qualified meeting is a meeting with a person who matches that description and who has agreed to discuss your service.

Do I need a CRM?add

No. We can work from a spreadsheet. If you already use HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive or Salesforce, we add every lead and meeting to it.

What happens if I cancel?add

After the 3-month minimum term, you can cancel with 30 days' written notice. Within 10 business days we send you an Excel file with all your contacts, conversations and meetings. We do not provide migration or integration services.

Is cold email allowed in the UAE and India?add

We contact business email addresses only, include an unsubscribe option in every email, and keep a permanent do-not-contact list. Our process is designed around the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Our guide on cold email rules explains both laws in detail.
