Data protection

The UAE Personal Data Protection Law

Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (the PDPL) governs personal data in the UAE. As of 2026, its detailed executive regulations have not yet been issued.

Companies registered in the DIFC or ADGM free zones follow their own data protection laws instead of the federal law.
