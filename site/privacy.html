Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data Ripe Piper collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it and how you can control it.

Last updated: 19 October 2026Draft for legal review

Who we are

Ripe Piper ([legal entity name], [registered address], United Arab Emirates) is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact us at hello@ripepiper.com with any privacy question.

Data we collect from website visitors

When you complete a form on this website, we collect your name, work email, company name, company website, country and the answers you give. We also collect basic usage data, such as the pages you visit, through privacy-friendly analytics.

Data we process for our clients

To run our service, we process the business contact details of people our clients want to reach: name, job title, company, business email address and public business information. We collect this data from our clients' own records and from public business sources. We do not buy contact lists.

Why we use the data

  • To reply to your request and book the meeting you asked for.
  • To send the calculator breakdown once, if you asked for it. We send no follow-up emails from the calculator.
  • To contact business prospects on behalf of our clients, with an unsubscribe option in every message.
  • To keep a do-not-contact list so that people who opt out are never contacted again.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent for website forms and on legitimate business interest for B2B outreach to business addresses. You can withdraw consent or object at any time.

How long we keep data

We keep website inquiries for 24 months after our last contact with you. We keep prospect data for the length of the client's contract plus 90 days. We keep do-not-contact entries permanently, because deleting them would allow the person to be contacted again.

Sharing and transfers

We share data only with service providers we need to run our service, such as email, hosting and calendar providers, under written agreements. Some providers store data outside the UAE or India. We choose providers that offer appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@ripepiper.com or use our opt-out page. We reply within 30 days and honour opt-out requests within 48 hours.

Security

We protect data with access controls, encrypted connections and regular backups. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.
