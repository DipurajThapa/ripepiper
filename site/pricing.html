Ownership

Choose who owns the system.

Both options use the same process. The difference is where the system runs and who owns the sending domains.

cloud_done

Managed (standard)

We run the system, the sending domains and the inboxes for you. You receive meetings and reports. This option has the lower setup fee.

domain

Client-owned

We install the system in your company's own accounts, and you own the sending domains. The setup fee is about twice the Managed setup fee, and the monthly fee is lower. We confirm both fees in your proposal.

You can cancel after the 3-month minimum term with 30 days' written notice. We then send you an Excel file with all your contacts, conversations and meetings within 10 business days. We do not provide migration or integration services, and under the Managed plan the sending domains and inboxes stay with us. Read the full exit policy.

Pricing questions

Questions about price and contracts.

Why do you publish your prices?add

Publishing prices saves time for both sides. You can check whether the service fits your budget before you book a call.

Why is there a 3-month minimum term?add

New sending inboxes need about two weeks to warm up, and results improve as we learn which messages work for your buyers. Three months gives enough time to show real results.

Do prices include VAT or GST?add

No. Prices on this page exclude VAT in the UAE and GST in India. Tax is added to your invoice where it applies.

Can I change packages?add

Yes. On the monthly engine you can switch between Revive, Signal, Response and Events at the start of any month.
