Ripe Signal

Find relevant prospects through business changes

Hiring, expansion or a new market can give you a reason to investigate a company. Ripe Signal helps your team review that change against your offer and decide whether a conversation is relevant. A business change alone does not prove a need or permission to contact.

  • check_circleYou sell a service or product to other businesses with deals worth AED 10,000 or ₹2.5 lakh or more.
  • check_circleYou need a steady flow of new customers every month, not only from referrals.
  • check_circleYou can describe the companies and job titles you want to meet.
What we need from you

Three things to get started.

  • assignmentA description of your ideal customer: industry, size, location and job titles.
  • assignmentThe business changes that may create a need for your offer. We agree which sources and signals are relevant during setup.
  • assignmentTwo or three customer examples you have permission to use, so any proposed message can draw on verified results.
Workflow

From a business change to a reviewed prospect.

Define

Agree your offer, target accounts, relevant changes and permitted sources. Confirm what needs to be connected before setting a start date.

Review

Review each dated source and contact identity. Compare the evidence with your client profile and keep missing or conflicting information visible.

Approve

Review the proposed approach and message before outreach. Sending requires separate contact authority and verified delivery setup.

Learn

Record accepted and rejected prospects, conversations and verified outcomes against the relevant signal. Use those findings to improve the next review.

A clear basis for each prospecting decision.

  • inventoryVisible gaps, expired signals and reasons to exclude a prospect
  • inventoryRecorded decisions and outcomes, with a review cadence agreed during setup

Questions

Common questions about Ripe Signal.

When can outreach start?add

We agree a start date after checking source access, contact authority, sender configuration and calendar readiness. Booking a consultation does not activate outreach, and a fixed number of meetings is not guaranteed.

Where do you find the signals?add

Sources are selected and approved for each client. These can include permitted company announcements, job postings or a supplied feed. We check relevance and permitted use before including evidence; public availability does not establish permission to contact.

How is the prospecting scope agreed?add

We agree the target market, source coverage, review capacity and any outreach limits after consultation. Quality and permission come before volume. A relevant signal is a reason to review a prospect, not a promise of a sale.
