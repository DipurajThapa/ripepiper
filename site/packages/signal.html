Questions

Common questions about Ripe Signal.

Why do the first meetings take three to four weeks?add

New sending inboxes need about two weeks of gradual use before they can safely send cold email. Sending sooner would put your messages at risk of landing in spam folders.

Where do you find the signals?add

We use public sources such as job boards, company websites and business news. We do not buy contact lists.

How many companies do you contact each month?add

It depends on your market size and how strict your ideal customer description is. We agree a monthly target on the kick-off call and report against it.
