Ripe Revive

Turn your old inquiries into booked meetings in 14 days

Many people who asked for a quote and then went quiet still need what you sell. Ripe Revive contacts them again with a short, relevant message and books the interested ones on your calendar.

Who it is for

Ripe Revive fits you if:

  • check_circleYou have 300 or more past inquiries, quotes or customers in a CRM or spreadsheet.
  • check_circleMost of those leads have not heard from you in the last 90 days.
  • check_circleA typical deal is worth at least AED 10,000 or ₹2.5 lakh.
What we need from you

Three things to get started.

  • assignmentAn export of your past leads with name, company, email and the date of last contact.
  • assignmentNotes on why each deal stalled, if you have them.
  • assignmentYour calendar link and the rules for a qualified meeting.
Timeline

How Ripe Revive runs, step by step.

Days 1–2

You send the export. We clean it, verify every address and remove anyone who opted out.

Day 3

We group leads by how far they got (inquiry, quote, meeting, past customer) and write one message for each group. You approve them.

Days 4–13

We send the messages, follow up twice and book every interested buyer on your calendar.

Day 14

We review the results with you. If we booked fewer than two qualified meetings, we refund the sprint fee.

Questions

Common questions about Ripe Revive.

What happens after the 14-day sprint?add

You can stop, or continue on the monthly engine. If you continue, we credit the sprint fee against your first month.

Will my old leads find this annoying?add

We send at most three short messages to each lead over about 10 days. Each message refers to their original inquiry, and anyone who replies 'no' or unsubscribes is never contacted again.

Which email address do the messages come from?add

Under the Managed plan, messages come from a separate domain that we set up for you, with a named sender from your company. Replies reach us and you at the same time.
