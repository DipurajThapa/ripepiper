Terms of service

These terms apply to every client of Ripe Piper. Your signed proposal confirms the package, the fees and the start date.

Last updated: 19 October 2026Draft for legal review

The service

We provide the lead generation package described in your signed proposal. We will make reasonable efforts to book qualified meetings, but we do not guarantee a specific number of meetings or sales, except for the Revive sprint refund described below.

Fees and payment

Fees are listed on our pricing page and confirmed in your proposal. Prices exclude VAT and GST. Monthly fees are invoiced in advance and are due within 7 days of the invoice date.

Revive sprint refund

If we book fewer than two qualified meetings during a 14-day Revive sprint, we refund the sprint fee in full within 14 days of the final review call. A qualified meeting is a meeting that takes place with a person who matches the ideal customer description agreed in writing at the start of the sprint.

Minimum term and cancellation

The monthly engine has a minimum term of 3 months. After the minimum term, either side can cancel with 30 days' written notice.

Data export on exit

When the service ends, we send you an Excel file with your contacts, conversations and meetings within 10 business days. We do not provide migration or integration services. The full details are in our exit policy.

Your responsibilities

You confirm that you have the right to share the lead data you give us, that your service is lawful, and that you will approve or reject draft messages within two business days.

Our responsibilities

We follow the rules described on our How it works page, keep your data confidential and use it only to provide your service.

Liability

Our total liability under these terms is limited to the fees you paid us in the three months before the claim. Neither side is liable for indirect losses, such as lost profit.

Governing law
